Review your session.

When you finish, a review screen shows how the session went and lets you tidy up before it is saved.

How it works

  1. Review and end.See your total climbs, sends and top grade in one clean summary.
  2. Gym location.Set or correct the gym name, with quick chips for places you climb often.
  3. Tweak ratings.Open any climb to fix its wall angle or adjust exertion, finger and body scores.
  4. Save immediately.Or choose End immediately to save without changing a thing.

Prefer to skip it? End immediately saves the session as it is.
