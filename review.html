Review your session.

When you finish, a review screen shows how the session went and lets you tidy up before it is saved.

How it works

  1. End your session and choose Review & end session.
  2. See your climbs, sends and top grade in one summary.
  3. Set or correct the gym name, with quick chips for places you climb a lot.
  4. Open any climb to fix its wall angle and adjust exertion, finger and body ratings, then tap Complete & Save Session.

Prefer to skip it? End immediately saves the session as it is.
