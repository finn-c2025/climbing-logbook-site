01 / 08 · Log

Log a climb in seconds.

Start a session at your gym, then log each climb one question at a time. Big buttons, one choice per screen, no typing.

How it works

  1. Start a session.A live timer, your climb count and your sends update as you go.
  2. Pick the grade.Choose from VB up to V18, or switch to the Fontainebleau scale from 3 to 8B.
  3. Log the outcome.Sent, Flash, Attempt or Project, then add your attempts and wall angle.
  4. Rate the feel.Rate exertion, finger feeling and body feel out of 10, and save.

Made a mistake? Undo last removes the latest climb. You can switch between V-Scale and Font grades in settings.
