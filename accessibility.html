Accessibility

We want this site to work for everyone. It aims to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA.

  • Works with keyboard and screen readers; all content is real text.
  • Respects your device's "reduce motion" setting: the pinned animation is replaced by a simple scrolling layout.
  • Adapts to phones and tablets and to zoomed text.

The animated phone mock-up is decorative; the same information is in the written feature cards. If you find a problem, email [YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS] and we will try to fix it.
