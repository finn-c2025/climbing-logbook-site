Mountain range & history.

Your archive draws every session as a peak, forming a skyline of your progression you can scrub through and inspect in depth.

How it works

  1. Peaks for sessions.Press and hold the archive icon on Home to open landscape peaks arranged by week.
  2. Scrub the skyline.Slide smoothly along the mountain range to preview dates, gyms, and top grades.
  3. Detailed climb list.Tap any session to see every logged problem with outcomes, attempts, and wall angles.
  4. Session comparison.Swipe down on any day to see rank, feel averages, and percentage changes vs previous sessions.

Session duration is shown in hours and minutes (e.g. 1.2h), making short and long sessions instantly comparable.
