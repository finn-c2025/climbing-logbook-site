05 / 08 · Style

Know your style.

Style Radar turns your logged climbs into a shape, so you can see how you climb at a glance.

How it works

  1. Log climbs as usual, including the wall angle.
  2. Open the Lab from the Home or Session screen.
  3. Switch to the Style Radar tab, next to Projects.
  4. Compare the shape over time as you log more sessions.

The more you log, the clearer your shape becomes. Style Radar lives in the Lab, where new ideas are tried first.
