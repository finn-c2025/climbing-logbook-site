05 / 08 · Style

Know your style.

Style Radar turns your logged climbs into a shape, so you can see how you climb at a glance.

How it works

  1. Log wall angles.Record Slab, Vertical, Overhang and Roof as you climb.
  2. Open the Lab.Find prototypes and experimental visualizations right inside the app.
  3. Inspect your radar.See where your volume and sends cluster across angles and hold types.
  4. Watch it evolve.Compare shapes over time as you climb more terrain.

The more you log, the clearer your shape becomes. Style Radar lives in the Lab, where new ideas are tried first.
