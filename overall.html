Overall stats.

One page that adds up everything you have logged: your hardest send, how often you send, and how you felt.

How it works

  1. One swipe away.From Home, swipe sideways or tap Swipe for stats.
  2. Hardest send & send rate.Hero numbers count up smoothly at the top of the screen.
  3. Feel breakdown.Average exertion, finger feeling and body feel across your training.
  4. 30-day focus.Stats automatically focus on your last 30 days when active, reflecting current form.

Stats use your last 30 days when you have recent sessions, so they reflect how you are climbing now.
