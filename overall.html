Overall stats.

One page that adds up everything you have logged: your hardest send, how often you send, and how you felt.

How it works

  1. From Home, swipe sideways or tap Swipe for stats.
  2. See your hardest send and send rate at the top, with the numbers counting up.
  3. Check the Feel section for your average exertion, fingers and body.
  4. Scroll the overview: average and peak grade, climbs sent, flash rate, average attempts, sessions in the last 30 days, time on the wall, average session and steep terrain.

Stats use your last 30 days when you have recent sessions, so they reflect how you are climbing now.
