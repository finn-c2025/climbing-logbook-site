Keep your projects.

Log a climb as a Project to break it into moves, track how far you get, and keep your beta in one place.

How it works

  1. Set up a project.Name it, pick the grade and wall angle, and choose up to 25 moves.
  2. Name the sequence.Label each move—Crimp, Pinch, Heel Hook—so the sequence makes sense to you.
  3. Track high point.Tap a move to mark progress. Reach the final move and it counts as a send.
  4. Record burns & beta.Add a burn after each attempt and keep your beta notes together.

Project climbs show in your session list with their move count and high point, and tapping one jumps straight back into it.
