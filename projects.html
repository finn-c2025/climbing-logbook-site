04 / 08 · Projects

Keep your projects.

Log a climb as a Project to break it into moves, track how far you get, and keep your beta in one place.

How it works

  1. Set up a project: name it, pick the grade and wall angle, and choose how many moves it has, up to 25.
  2. Name each move so the sequence makes sense to you.
  3. Tap a move to set your high point. Reach the final move and it counts as a send.
  4. Add a burn after each attempt, write beta notes, and use Log Send or Undo Send as the project changes.

Project climbs show in your session list with their move count and high point, and tapping one jumps straight back into it.
