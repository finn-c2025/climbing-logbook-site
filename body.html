Body measures.

Every climb can carry three simple 1 to 10 ratings, so you can look back and see how your body actually felt.

How it works

  1. Exertion: how hard the climb felt overall.
  2. Fingers: how your fingers felt, useful for spotting overload early.
  3. Body: how your body felt, from shoulders to core.
  4. Averages for all three appear together as the Feel section of your stats.

Ratings are quick to set with sliders in the review screen, and you can change them after the session.
