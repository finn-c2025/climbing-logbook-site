Body measures.

Every climb can carry three simple 1 to 10 ratings, so you can look back and see how your body actually felt.

How it works

  1. Exertion.How hard the climb felt overall, from warm-up ease to absolute limit.
  2. Fingers.How your tendons and pulleys felt, useful for spotting overload early.
  3. Body.How your core, shoulders and muscles held up across the session.
  4. Feel trends.Averages for all three appear together in your stats.

Ratings are quick to set with sliders in the review screen, and you can change them after the session.
