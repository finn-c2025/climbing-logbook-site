Session history and stats.

Look back at any session and see the numbers that matter, without digging through a spreadsheet.

How it works

  1. Scrub the skyline.Slide smoothly through your past sessions and inspect details on touch.
  2. Day overview.See the date, gym, session duration, and climb breakdown instantly.
  3. Climb list.Check every problem logged with its outcome, attempts, and wall angle.
  4. Comparison card.Swipe down to see session rank, feel ratings, and percentage changes vs previous days.

Session length is shown in hours and minutes, for example 1.2h, so short and long sessions are easy to compare.
