Session history and stats.

Look back at any session and see the numbers that matter, without digging through a spreadsheet.

How it works

  1. Open a session in the archive.
  2. See the date, the gym, the length of the session and how many climbs you sent.
  3. Check the top grade and the average grade for that day.
  4. Scroll through every climb you logged, with its outcome, attempts and wall angle.

Session length is shown in hours and minutes, for example 1.2h, so short and long sessions are easy to compare.
