06 / 08 · Mountains

Sessions as a mountain range.

Your archive draws every session as a peak, so your progress looks like a skyline.

How it works

  1. Press and hold the archive button on the Home screen.
  2. The archive opens in landscape.
  3. Each session is a peak. Harder top grades make taller peaks, and sessions are placed by week.
  4. Open a session to see its details, or delete a session or climb you no longer want.

It is designed to be glanced at: a rising skyline means you are climbing harder.
