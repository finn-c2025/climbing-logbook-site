06 / 08 · Mountains

Sessions as a mountain range.

Your archive draws every session as a peak, so your progress looks like a skyline.

How it works

  1. Open the archive.Press and hold the archive button on the Home screen to open in landscape.
  2. Peaks for sessions.Harder top grades make taller peaks, arranged naturally by week.
  3. Visual skyline.A rising skyline gives an instant, glanceable sense of your progression.
  4. Inspect any day.Tap any peak to open its breakdown, climb cards, or delete entries.

It is designed to be glanced at: a rising skyline means you are climbing harder.
